PM Codex is a project management app that runs on your own computer and keeps your data in your own Google Sheet. Claude, and the built-in Gemini assistant, read your project and propose changes. Nothing changes until you review them.

Modules

10 screens, one Google Sheet

Everything a project manager keeps, grouped the way the app's module bar groups it. The Overview pulls the late, the blocked and the due-this-week from all of them. These are the app's own screens; each one opens in the demo above.

Change requests

2 assistants, 1 review

PM Codex works with two assistants: Claude, from the Claude desktop app, and Gemini, built into the app. Neither one writes to your project. Whatever an assistant wants to change arrives as a change request, and every line in it shows what the value was and what it becomes.

  1. 01

    The assistant reads

    Either assistant sees the project the way you do: modules, records and the Codex.

  2. 02

    It files one request

    Every change it wants goes into a single change request, waiting for you in the app.

  3. 03

    You review

    Each line opens on the record it changes, old value above new. Edit it, or untick it to decline.

  4. 04

    You sign off

    The ticked lines apply and save to your Sheet. The assistant is told what you approved and what you didn't.

Both assistants' requests use the same review panel and the same four marks, in every module. A line you edit before signing off stays yours: no assistant can overwrite it with a new proposal.

They also share one standing brief: read first, ask before assuming, keep it short. Each picks a cartridge for the problem in front of it and logs it.

Privacy

Your machine, your Sheet
The app
Runs on your own computer at http://localhost:7800, installed and started by Pinokio. Nothing is hosted for you.
Your data
Lives in a Google Sheet you own, through an Apps Script you deploy yourself. The Sheet keeps its own version history.
The Gemini key
Stays in the small local server that forwards Gemini calls. It never reaches the page, and the server refuses to start if a build ever carries it.
The archive
Finished tasks, deliverables and risks you archive drop out of what the AI sees by default. It can still read them when it asks.

Privacy glossary

List the names you'd rather keep to yourself. The app marks them as you type and swaps them for tokens before anything reaches the AI, then swaps them back in the answer.

Install

Mac now, Windows later

What you need

  • A Mac
  • Pinokio, the free app that installs and runs local apps
  • A Google account, for your own Sheet
  • A Gemini API key (optional)
  • The Claude desktop app (optional)

Install and start

  1. 01

    Open PM Codex on Pinokio, or in Pinokio open Discover and search for PM Codex. You can also download from this address:

    https://github.com/Ashvall01/PM-Codex
  2. 02

    Click Install.

  3. 03

    Click Start. Pinokio asks for a Gemini key; leave it empty to skip Gemini.

  4. 04

    Click Open PM Codex. The first time, the setup wizard walks you through making your Sheet and connecting it.

Connect to Claude

  1. 01Install the Claude desktop app.
  2. 02In Pinokio, PM Codex, click Connect to Claude, then Install in the window Claude shows.
  3. 03Keep PM Codex open. In a new Claude conversation, say “Start a PM Codex session.”

Three rules

  1. 1One browser tab only. Each tab saves your whole Sheet, so two tabs overwrite each other.
  2. 2Always use http://localhost:7800. A different address starts an empty app.
  3. 3Keep the Sheet's web app address private. Anyone who has it can read your data.
