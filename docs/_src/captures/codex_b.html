How dates move

A target date changes only after steering agrees, and the reason goes in the task's remarks.

  • Go-live is fixed. Anything that threatens it is a risk, not a new date.
  • An issue without an owner gets one within a working day.
  • Finance signs off anything that touches the chart of accounts.
